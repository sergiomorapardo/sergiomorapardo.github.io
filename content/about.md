I am an AI/ML Technical Lead and Senior Data Scientist with more than seven years of experience building and scaling production machine learning systems.

My work sits at the intersection of **modeling, product experimentation, and systems engineering**. I have led recommendation and ranking initiatives, causal ML programs, production serving architectures, and evaluation methods for applied LLM systems.

## How I approach ML products

I start with the decision the product needs to make and the evidence required to know whether that decision helped. From there, I design the data, model, experiment, serving path, and monitoring loop as one connected system.

An accurate model that cannot be deployed, observed, or causally evaluated is not yet a successful ML product.

## Teaching and communication

Since 2023 I have taught graduate and specialization courses at Pontificia Universidad Javeriana, covering deep learning, NLP, graph ML, computer vision, classical ML, time series, Monte Carlo methods, anomaly detection, and LLMs.
